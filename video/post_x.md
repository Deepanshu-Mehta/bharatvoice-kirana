Kiranas get calls like this. I built Munshi ji: AI that answers the phone in Hinglish & Haryanvi, writes the parchi, remembers you, calls when you run low.

Using @GnaniAi Prisma, Timbre & Evon

---
How it works, on @GnaniAi:

🎙️ Prisma hears Hinglish, biased to the brands in the call
🗣️ Timbre is Munshi ji's voice, streamed as it speaks
🧠 Evon v3.3 turns "woh meethi safed cheez" into cheeni

The LLM proposes, code decides. Nothing is ordered without your "haan".
---
Honest numbers (synthetic callers, noisy 8 kHz phone line):

✅ Orders exactly right: Hindi 100%, Haryanvi 90%, Hinglish 84%
📉 Questions per call as it learns a regular: 3.3 → 1.4
🧾 Udhaar goes straight into the khata

Every number comes from a script in the repo.

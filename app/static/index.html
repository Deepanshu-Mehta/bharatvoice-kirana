What happens in one turn

  1. Hear

    Prisma v3.0 transcribes, primed with the brands in your order and the question the shop just asked.

  2. Understand

    Rules handle everyday phrasing in milliseconds. Evon v3.3 steps in for lines like “woh meethi safed cheez jo chai mein daalte hain”.

  3. Decide

    Code matches the catalog, checks stock and udhaar limits, and decides whether to ask. The model never sets a price.

  4. Reply

    Timbre answers in your own way of speaking, streamed so the first word arrives in about half a second.

On the shopkeeper's side of the counter

WhatsApp voice notes become draft orders with one tap. Udhaar is written in the khata with a credit limit the code enforces, and the shop can send a polite reminder in the customer's own way of speaking. Open the shop counter.
